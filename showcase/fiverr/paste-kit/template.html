Fiverr rules this kit follows

  • New sellers can have 4 gigs, drafts included. Level 1 and 2 get 10.
  • About: 150 to 600 characters, with no URLs, company names, or contact details.
  • Title: letters, numbers, spaces, and punctuation only. Fiverr rejected "&", so the live title says "and".
  • Gig descriptions: up to 1,200 characters. A gig may link your portfolio or website, never contact details.
  • Portfolio: 1 to 20 projects, up to 5 files each, with a description of 120 to 1,400 characters.
  • Intro video: 20 to 60 seconds, at least 1280×720, with you on camera.
  • Gig video: up to 75 seconds, at least 1280×720, landscape, MP4. You don't need to be in it.
  • Profile photo: a clear photo of you or your own logo. Nobody else's logo, no celebrities, no GIFs.
