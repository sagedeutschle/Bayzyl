## Fiverr rules (checked against help.fiverr.com)

- New sellers get 4 gigs, drafts included. Level 1 and Level 2 sellers get 10.
- About: 150 to 600 characters. No URLs, company names, or contact details.
- Title: letters, numbers, spaces, and punctuation only. "&" is rejected. Display name: 3 to 15 characters.
- Profile photo: a clear photo of yourself, or your own logo. Not someone else's logo, not a celebrity, not a GIF.
- Intro video: optional. 20 to 60 seconds, at least 1280×720. Sage must be on camera and speaking, with nobody else in it.
- Gig video: optional. Up to 75 seconds, at least 1280×720 at 16:9, landscape, MP4 or AVI. No face needed.
- Portfolio: 1 to 20 projects, up to 5 files each, with a 120 to 1,400 character description.
- Editing About: Dashboard → View profile in Selling mode → the pencil beside the field.
  On 10-01 there was no pencil on About. Fiverr says to finish any open onboarding tasks on
  the dashboard first. The onboarding page is where the bot check started.
