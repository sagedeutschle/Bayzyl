## title
Helix Research Desk

## subtitle

A local-first desk for source-backed genetics research

## status

Local research workbench

## year
2026

## role
Design + engineering

## summary

A local-first macOS workbench for personal, non-clinical genetics research. Its source adapters bring public evidence into a common view with identity checks, provenance, dates and visible conflicts. A local notebook keeps research snapshots and notes.

The project separates personal genotype imports from public-source research and makes optional agent handoffs explicit. It is a research and education tool, not a diagnosis or treatment service.

## facts

- Platform: macOS desktop
- Evidence: Named public-source adapters
- Storage: Local notebook and cache
- Scope: Non-clinical research

## highlights

- Identity anchors and provenance-aware verification
- Conflicting and insufficient evidence remain visible
- Local import workflows with explicit public-query boundaries
- Reviewable public-only briefs before optional agent handoffs
