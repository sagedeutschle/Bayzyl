## summary
Shorepact is a small collection of tabletop games built around a coast. In the original game, collect resources, connect settlements and trade for your next move. Harbor Charter turns the waterfront into a contest of auctions and property. Breakwater Run asks when to bank a dice roll and when to risk more. Lantern Bluff puts hidden dice and bold claims around the same table.

The native 1.1 candidate brings these games together on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Eleven visual themes carry through the menus and play, from Block Print and Painted Wood to neon-lit boards. The images here show real native views; the Mac scenes use sample game state. Apple availability follows review. The separate browser playtest requires an invitation and currently covers the original Shorepact game.

## story.decision.1.title
Let the art travel with the game

## story.decision.1.text
The selected theme follows the player from the clubhouse to the table. Terrain, cards, dice and interface colors belong together, so choosing a style changes the feeling of the whole evening.

## story.decision.2.title
Keep the rules specific to each table

## story.decision.2.text
Four games share a home without pretending to play alike. The original is a building-and-trading game; Harbor Charter centers on property; Breakwater Run is about risk; Lantern Bluff is about incomplete information.

Explore the native collection above, or enter the invitation-only browser playtest for the original Shorepact. Crossplay requires compatible clients; the three companion games currently stay on one device.
