## summary
Savepoint explores a studio for connecting ideas, writing, worldbuilding and drawing. Keep related pieces together and move between a project’s notes, characters, scenes and canvases. A thought can stay small, grow into a story or become part of a larger world.

These images are previews of real native interface components, rendered with a sample project in November 2026. They show the design in development, rather than a complete application session or a publicly released product.
