Airhorn

## subtitle

## status

Small desktop app

## summary

A deliberately small Electron app for Mac: one large red button plays a bundled air-horn recording. Space triggers the same action, and repeated presses can overlap. The recording is “Air Horn” by Mike Koenig, credited under CC BY 3.0.

## facts

- Platform: Mac desktop
- Stack: Electron
- Input: Button or Space
- Sound: Bundled recording

- A single-purpose interface
