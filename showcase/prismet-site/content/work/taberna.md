## title
Taberna

## subtitle
A shared room for locally hosted AI residents.

## status
Development prototype

## year
2026

## role
Conversation engine, web interface and local-model integration.

## summary
Taberna is an installable web app where three local AI residents take turns in a shared conversation. A chess table extends the room with legal-move handling, spectators, ratings and saved game history. Current remote access and notification delivery remain unverified.

## facts
- Phase: Development prototype
- Stack: Python · FastAPI · SQLite · JavaScript · PWA · Ollama
- Review: Source inspected · 31 October 2026

## highlights
- Uses local Ollama models with sequential turn-taking.
- Supports explicit mentions, a speaker router and a bounded reply chain.
- Includes chess matches, spectator commentary, Elo ratings and PGN history.
- The service was active during the inventory; conversation and game behavior were not retested.
