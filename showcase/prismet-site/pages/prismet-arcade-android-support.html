Prismet Arcade Android Support

Android edition · Published by Sage Deutschle

Need help or found a bug? Email sagedeutschle@pm.me.

Include your Android device model, Android version, Prismet Arcade version, game name and what happened. A screenshot can help. Do not send passwords, payment details or private files.

Common questions

What is included in the Android edition?

The collection contains 20 offline games: Wordgame, Cube Puzzle, 2048, Lights Out, Minesweeper, Snake, Sudoku, Sliding-15, Nonogram, Chess, Reversi, Connect Four, Checkers, Gomoku, Sea Battle, Settler Scramble, Solitaire, Crazy 8, Spider and Brick Bench.

The Android edition is designed for one upfront purchase with its games included. It has no ads, app accounts or in-app purchases.

How do I start playing?

Choose a game from the collection. Search by its name or category, or mark a favorite to find it again. Each game has a How to play panel with its controls.

Do I need a connection or an account?

The Android games work offline and do not require an app account. This edition does not include online multiplayer or cloud sync. Supported board games provide bot or pass-and-play controls within the game.

How do daily puzzles work?

Daily mode uses the collection’s own seeded puzzles and changes at midnight UTC. Wordgame uses an independent bundled word collection; it does not fetch an external publisher’s daily answer.

How do I keep or restore a save?

Progress saves locally on your Android device. Open Keep your place within a game to export a save through Android’s document picker. To restore a compatible file, choose Import save. The app checks it and asks before replacing the visible session.

Keep your own exported copy before clearing app storage, uninstalling or moving to another device. Exports remain in the folder or provider you selected. A save file is a separate copy, not automatic cloud sync.

A game board does not load.

Update Android System WebView or Chrome through Google Play, then reopen Prismet Arcade. If the problem remains, email the device and game details above. Export your saves before clearing app storage or reinstalling.

How do I delete local data?

Clear Prismet Arcade’s storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. Delete any exported files separately from the locations where you saved them.

How is my information handled?

Read the Prismet Arcade Android Privacy Policy for local saves, document providers, Google Play and support-email details.
