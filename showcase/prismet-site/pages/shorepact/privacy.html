Shorepact Privacy Policy

Shorepact for iPhone and Mac · Published by Sage Deutschle · Last updated November 1, 2026

Shorepact is a hex-tile building and trading board game. It is built to need as little data as possible. This page explains what the app does with data, in plain terms.

The short version

  • No account, email address or phone number is required to play.
  • The developer collects no data from the app. There is no analytics, crash reporting or advertising in Shorepact.
  • The app does not track you and does not request permission to track you.
  • We do not sell personal information.

What stays on your device

Your current table, the names and playing styles of the bots at your table, your chosen theme and board view, your idle-animation setting and your last table setup are stored on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Game Center and online play

Playing with friends is optional and uses Apple’s Game Center. When you invite or join friends, Apple’s GameKit sends game moves between the players’ devices, and the other players at your table can see your Game Center display name. The app may use your local network to connect with nearby players. Game Center is governed by Apple’s privacy policy. Shorepact does not run its own game server and does not receive your Apple ID, your game moves or your player information.

iCloud saves

If you are signed in to Game Center, you can choose to save a table to iCloud and restore it later. These saves are stored by Apple in your account’s saved games. They are not sent to the developer.

This website

Like any website, prismet.xyz may appear in routine server logs (for example your IP address and the time of a request) at our hosting provider, Fly.io. The Shorepact privacy and support pages contain no scripts, cookies or trackers.

Children

Shorepact contains no chat, no user-generated content, no ads and no purchases. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

Because the app collects nothing, there is nothing for us to delete or export. To clear local data, delete the app. To manage Game Center data or iCloud saves, use your Apple account settings.

Changes

If our practices change, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date before the change takes effect.
