Shorepact Support

Shorepact for iPhone and Mac · Published by Sage Deutschle

Need help or found a bug? Email sagedeutschle@pm.me.

Please include your device (iPhone or Mac model), the operating system version, the Shorepact version, the table setup you chose, and what happened. Screenshots help. Do not send passwords or payment details.

Common questions

How do I start a game?

Tap Play with bots, choose how many seats and the rules you want, then tap Start table with bots. You play against one to three bots. The in-game How to play guide explains rolling, building, trading and winning.

Do I need an account?

No. Solo play against bots needs no account. Game Center is optional and only used to play with friends and to save tables to iCloud.

How do I play with friends?

Choose Play with friends and sign in to Game Center when asked. Each player needs their own Game Center account. iPhone and Mac players can share a table.

An online table disconnected.

If a player or the host loses their connection, the table pauses and the connection ends. Automatic reconnect is not available, so start a new table. Online tables do not fill empty seats with bots.

My table or settings disappeared.

Your table is saved on your device. Deleting or reinstalling the app, or restoring a different device without a backup, can remove it. If you saved a table to iCloud while signed in to Game Center, open Cloud saves on the home screen to restore it.

Can I turn off the animation?

Yes. Open Settings and turn Idle animations off. You can also switch between a flat overhead board and a raised board, and pick one of the table themes. These settings are personal to your device.
