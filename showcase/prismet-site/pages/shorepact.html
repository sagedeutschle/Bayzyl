Small island. Many possibilities.

Every turn leaves
its mark.

Turn brick, lumber, wool, grain, and ore into a growing foothold. Connect your settlements, develop your cities, and decide when to build, when to trade, and when to save for a bigger move.

Shorepact brings the feel of a tabletop to a native game: textured pieces, illustrated cards, and a board you can view from above or raise into a miniature landscape.

01

Rivals with character

Play solo against bots with different priorities, from builders and explorers to merchants and opportunists.

02

Your table, your pace

Choose Relaxed, Tabletop, or Quick pacing for solo play. Adjust the island, victory goal, and starting supplies.

03

Make yourself at home

Explore eleven visual themes, switch between flat and raised boards, and learn with an optional step-by-step tutorial.
