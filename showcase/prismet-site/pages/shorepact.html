Four games.
Find your favorite.

The original Shorepact is a race to make a coast your own. Gather brick, lumber, wool, grain and ore. Connect settlements, develop cities and trade for the move that changes everything.

Learn with an optional First Landing tutorial, play solo against bots, or gather two to four players online using compatible clients. See the island from above or raise it into a miniature landscape.

Harbor Charter

Buy, auction and trade waterfront titles. Build a harbor fortune while watching what your rivals can afford.

Breakwater Run

Roll for cargo, bank your haul or push your luck. Eight rounds give every risk a little more weight.

Lantern Bluff

Keep your dice hidden, read the bids and choose the moment to challenge. A small table with plenty to guess.

The project

A place at the table.

Shorepact is an independent Prismet collection by Sage Deutschle. The native 1.1 candidate contains all four games. Apple availability follows review; the browser playtest is a separate invitation-only release.

At the table
Original Shorepact: 2–4 seats, solo bots or compatible online clients. Harbor Charter, Breakwater Run and Lantern Bluff: 2–6 seats on one device, with humans or bots.
Online development
The original Shorepact supports private table codes and random matchmaking across compatible clients. The three companion games are local. An online table ends if a player disconnects.
Steam plans
Exploring a Steam release. Platforms, pricing, and release date are not yet announced.
