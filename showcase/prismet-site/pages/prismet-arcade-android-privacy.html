Prismet Arcade Android Privacy Policy

Android edition · Published by Sage Deutschle · Last updated November 3, 2026

This policy covers the Android app Prismet Arcade, identified as xyz.prismet.arcade. This edition contains 20 offline games. Its data practices are described here separately from other Prismet apps and editions.

What the Android app collects

  • The app does not collect or send personal information or game data to the developer.
  • It has no internet permission, advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • There are no app accounts, online multiplayer services or in-app purchases.
  • The developer does not sell or share personal information collected by this app, because the app collects none.

What stays on your device

Game progress, moves, elapsed play time, daily completion records, favorites and preferences such as your theme are stored in the app’s local storage. The app uses this information to keep your place and apply your choices. It does not run a cloud-save service or upload this information to us.

The game interface is bundled with the app and displayed using Android System WebView. Its local asset address is not an online account or game server.

Files you choose to export or import

You can export a game save or a Brick Bench parts file, or select a compatible save to import, using Android’s document picker. The app accesses only the document you choose for that operation. When preparing an export, it also creates a temporary local copy inside the app’s private cache.

An exported copy goes to the folder or document provider you select. The developer does not receive it. If you choose a cloud-storage provider or share the file yourself, that provider or recipient handles the copy under its own practices. Keep exported saves somewhere you trust.

Purchases and platform services

If you obtain the app through Google Play, Google processes the upfront purchase and related store transaction. The app does not ask you to enter payment-card information and has no in-app billing SDK. Google’s handling of store, purchase and developer payment records is governed by its own terms and privacy practices.

Android, Google Play, your device and any document provider you choose may process information under their own policies. Those platform services are separate from the app’s local game data.

Keeping and deleting your data

Local game data stays on your device until you clear Prismet Arcade’s storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. Files you export are separate copies and remain wherever you saved them; delete those copies from the selected locations if you no longer want them.

There is no Prismet Arcade Android account to delete and no developer-held game-data record to request. Your device or storage provider may offer its own backup or transfer features, which you control through its settings.

Support requests

If you email us for help, we receive the email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request. This is a separate communication you initiate, not automatic collection by the app. Do not send passwords, payment details or private files. You can ask us to delete a support message by contacting the address below; email providers may retain their own copies under their policies.

Children

The app’s local data practices apply to all players. It does not request a player’s name, email address, age or location, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

This website

Opening this webpage sends a normal web request to prismet.xyz. Our hosting provider, Fly.io, may retain routine request logs such as an IP address and request time. This Android privacy page and its companion support page contain no scripts, cookies or trackers. Visiting these pages is separate from playing the offline Android app.

Changes and contact

If the Android app’s data practices change, we will update this policy and its date.

For questions about Prismet Arcade for Android or this policy, email sagedeutschle@pm.me.
